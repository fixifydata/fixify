Frequently asked questions

Do you need admin access?

We usually begin with read-only access. Edit access is only needed for agreed fixes, and everything stays in your accounts.

Will you rebuild everything?

No. We preserve what works and recommend focused changes first.

How do you validate tracking?

We test the agreed journeys, consent states and downstream events against the relevant source systems.

Can you work with our agency or developers?

Yes. We document the work and can coordinate directly with your technical team.

Who owns the work?

You do. Your accounts, containers and documentation remain yours.
