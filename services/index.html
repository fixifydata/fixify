What we do

Measurement that moves your business forward.

Fixify builds the measurement layer between your marketing activity and your business decisions. We scope the questions first, implement the right signals, test them in their real platforms and leave your team with a system it can maintain.

GA4 Implementation & Audits

We turn business questions into a governed GA4 property with an event taxonomy, lead and ecommerce journeys, custom dimensions, audiences and integrations with CRM, Ads and warehouse tools. Audits reconcile GA4 against backend truth, surface missing parameters and produce a prioritized repair plan.

Google Tag Manager

We design a clear container structure, data layer requirements, tags, triggers, variables and release workflow. Preview-mode QA, permissions, naming rules and documentation make it easier to ship changes without creating duplicate or conflicting signals.

Conversion Tracking

We connect clicks to outcomes across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and other platforms. That includes enhanced conversions, purchase values, qualified lead stages, form and phone events, event identifiers, deduplication and validation against CRM or ecommerce records.

Attribution & Marketing Measurement

We create practical UTM governance and journey definitions, then align attribution windows, channel rules, pipeline stages and revenue views. The result is a measurement framework that helps teams compare campaigns without pretending every touchpoint is equally measurable.

Server-Side Tracking

We plan or improve server-side GTM and first-party delivery with event mapping, authentication, hashing, enrichment and browser/server deduplication. Consent states are carried through the design, and platform diagnostics are used to validate signal quality before handover.

Analytics & Reporting

We turn ad, analytics, CRM and offline data into reporting people can actually use. Engagements can include Looker Studio dashboards, BigQuery-ready models, metric definitions, source reconciliation and alerts that catch volume, spend or conversion breaks before the next review.

A consistent delivery model

Every service moves through discovery, a written scope with dependencies, implementation with your team, validation in the relevant platforms and a documented handover. That keeps the work useful after launch rather than leaving you with a status deck.
